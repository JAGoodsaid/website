B2B Client Onboarding With Claude and HubSpot

By Jasmine A. Goodsaid · Dealers United

What still needs to happen before a client is onboarded?

For a client operations team, the useful output of a CRM is often a next step. At Dealers United, I built a Claude artifact that turns CRM data into a shared onboarding task list.

How the workflow works

The artifact reads CRM information and uses it to build a task list the team can share. It also updates HubSpot automatically, connecting the list people work from with the system that holds the client record.

Where AI fits

The product decision was to give the team a shared list of actions. Claude is part of the implementation; the deliverable is something a teammate can use to see what needs attention. This is the kind of applied AI product work I want to keep doing.

Tools: Claude Artifacts · HubSpot · CRM integration · Workflow design
