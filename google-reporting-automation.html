Google Apps Script Automation for Ad Reporting

By Jasmine A. Goodsaid · Dealers United

How do you bring campaign data into one reporting workflow?

Ad platforms, streaming campaigns, and email programs each produce their own reporting data. At Dealers United, I built API integrations with Google Apps Script to sync those inputs into Google Sheets.

From source data to a shared sheet

I used Apps Script to connect the reporting sources to a common destination. That separated the data collection work from the reporting work: the integrations move the inputs, and the sheet gives the team a place to review them.

Why the distinction matters

Putting two channels in the same sheet doesn’t make their metrics equivalent. A streaming impression and an email interaction still describe different events. A shared reporting workflow gives the team a place to examine those differences before drawing a conclusion.
